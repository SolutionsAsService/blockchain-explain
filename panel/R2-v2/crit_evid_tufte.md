Notes
- Not objecting: the isometric depth in the hero. Stack order and the light-to-dark ramp carry the layer order. The slab sides are a small amount of non-data ink, but they make the hero match the kept logo, and they fit a "very basic" brief.
- Minor, optional: the numeric columns in the BFT table could be right-aligned (with tabular-nums already on), and the calculator output could repeat the table's wording exactly ("more than ⅔" / "fewer than ⅓"). Neither is blocking.
- The chain demo is the strongest graphic on the site: one design repeated, with the change marked directly on the mark. The Merkle demo should follow the same approach.
