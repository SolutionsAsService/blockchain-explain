1. **The label in the Consensus lead figure is covered.** At both 1280 and 375, the figure that opens the page reads "A CHEATER'S REWRIT": the dashed "Block 3′" box sits over the end of the label. It is the first thing a beginner reads on the page. `measure.cjs` misses it because the text is covered by another shape, not cut off at the edge. Fix: put "A cheater's rewrite" on its own line above the dashed blocks, the same way "The chain everyone has" sits above the top row. Alternatively, start the rewrite row further right. Also add a check to `measure.cjs` that text boxes do not overlap other shapes.
2. **Mining 5 zeros takes about a minute, with no estimate and no way to stop.** Zeros needed goes up to 5. At 5 the demo ran for 57 seconds (2,382,719 guesses) at 1280. During that time the button is disabled as "Mining…", the data field stays editable, and nothing says how long the wait will be. Fix: add a "Stop" button while mining. When zeros are 5, show "About a million guesses: this can take a minute" under the field, or cap the field at 4. Disable the Block data field while mining, or restart the search when it changes.
