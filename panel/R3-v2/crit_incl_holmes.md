Notes:
- Who is excluded: the "very basic" reader with no crypto background. "Stake" appears in the consensus page and in the glossary's own definition of Validator ("in proportion to its stake") without ever being explained, so the glossary is circular for exactly the reader it is meant to serve. "Forks to wait out" is both undefined jargon and an idiom that a non-native reader is unlikely to know.
- The hash demo is usable without knowing hex, because typing and watching the output change is enough. Still, the "64 of 64 digits match" comparison and the glossary's "64 hex digits" leave a non-technical reader wondering why letters count as digits. One parenthesis fixes that for everyone.
- Smaller points, not objections: "bytecode", "keys that sign" and "Evmos" on evm.html, and "packets/proofs" on layer0.html, are lightly explained. "Transaction" is never defined, but the examples make it clear. If the build adds more glossary entries, "Transaction" and "Fork" are the next most useful.
- Mace's WCAG mechanics (contrast and focus) were not re-checked, as instructed.
