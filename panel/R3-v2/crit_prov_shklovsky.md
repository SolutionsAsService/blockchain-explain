Notes
- Home graphic: PASS on its own terms. The bedrock, read as the ground that every layer and every chain rests on, is the estranging device. Do not add motion or glow.
- The hash diff ("2 of 64 digits match", with matches marked) is good defamiliarization. It makes "completely different" countable rather than a cliché.
- The re-link counter ("1 so far … the break moved to block 3") already turns the cost of hiding an edit into an action. With the connector fixed, the chain demo is complete.
- Nothing else is requested. More decoration would only make things familiar again.
- Evidence: Playwright screenshots in my scratchpad (broken.png, relink1.png) show the green connector staying intact above the red, dashed Block 2 and Block 3.
