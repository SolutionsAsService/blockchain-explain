1. **The "Level N of 4" numbers contradict the reading order.** Keys is the second page in the pager, nav and cards, but its kicker and "You are here" say "Level 4 of 4, where people start". The next page, Consensus, says "Level 1 of 4, the bottom", so a reader goes 4, 1, 2, 3. Fix: take "Level N of 4" out of the kickers and the `.side-where` text. Use the reading position plus the layer name instead, for example "Step 2 of 8 · Keys & wallets (top of the stack)" and "Step 3 of 8 · Consensus (bottom of the stack)". The stack drawing can still show where the layer sits.
2. **The sidebar map names things differently from everywhere else.** The same page is called "Keys" in the nav, "Keys and wallets" on the card and "Wallets & apps" on the stack slab and in "You are here". The bridge is a small unlabelled "↔" box with a dashed line that a beginner cannot read. Fix: label the top slab "Keys & wallets" in the sidebar map (and in the home hero, if the same slab is shared). Replace the "↔" box with the word "Bridges", as the home hero already does.
3. **The Consensus figure does not explain consensus at a glance.** The first thing under "In one line" is a 10x10 grid of green and red squares about BFT fault tolerance. That is section 03 of the page, and it says nothing about work or stake. A beginner reads it as "a third of the network is bad". Fix: move the grid down into section 03, next to the table and the validator calculator. Put a figure for the page's one idea at the top: two competing histories, with "rewrite costs: electricity (work) or deposit (stake)".
4. **The mine demo shows its answer before you press Mine, and shows a mismatched pair while mining.** On load, Nonce 1526 and the green 000e589… hash are already filled in. Pressing Mine with the defaults changes only "for 3 zeros", so nothing seems to happen. With 5 zeros, the nonce counts up (121,600…) next to the old green 000e589… hash, which looks like a found result. Fix: start with Nonce "—", Hash "—" and "Press Mine to start guessing". While mining, show the current trial hash in a muted colour (or "—"), change the button text to "Mining…", and turn the hash green only when a match is found.
5. **The validator calculator fails silently on bad input.** Typing 0 or clearing the field gives "Needed to finalize: 1 · faulty: 0". Typing 5000 gives the result for 1000 while the box still shows 5000. Fix: on input or blur, clamp the field's own value to 1–1000 and show "Enter a number from 1 to 1000" when it is out of range. Start the result line with the number used: "With 31 validators: 21 must sign · up to 10 can fail".
6. **The sign demo keeps a stale verdict and has two transaction boxes that drift apart.** After "Invalid…", editing box 1 leaves "Invalid" on screen even though the boxes now differ in a new way. Editing either box after "Valid" also leaves the old verdict. Valid and Invalid are plain black text that look the same. "Sign it" and "Check the signature" sit greyed out with no reason given. Fix: on any input in either box, replace the verdict with "Changed. Press Check the signature." Style the verdicts as green "✓ Valid" and red "✗ Invalid". Under the disabled buttons, add "Make a key pair first" and "Sign it first".
7. **"Re-link the broken block" stays clickable when nothing is broken**, both on load and after every link is repaired. Fix: disable it while the chain is whole, with the label "Nothing to repair: edit a block above". Enable it only when a block shows "broken".
8. **The Merkle demo says "watch the root" but gives nothing to compare against.** After you edit a transaction, every hash just changes. There is no old root and no mark showing which nodes changed. Fix: keep the original root under the live one ("was e39a9c57…"). Highlight, in the same marker style the hash demo uses, the leaf, the H(1+2) or H(3+4) node and the root that changed.
9. **At 375 px the "You are here" chip and the kicker repeat each other, and parts of the Basics figure are cut off.** On Basics, "The bedrock, under every level" appears twice in a row: in the chip, then in the kicker. On mobile the stack and "On this page" are hidden, so the chip adds nothing. The figure's arrow labels are cut at the right edge ("becomes the next "previous"). Fix: hide `.side-where` below the mobile breakpoint, or show a collapsed "On this page" list there instead. At narrow widths, wrap the arrow label onto two lines ("becomes the / next previous") or let the SVG scale down instead of cropping.
