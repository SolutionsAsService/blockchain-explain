# R6-v2 critique: Critic: Formalist (Greenberg)

| Critic | Anchor used | Observed in the work | Verdict PASS/OBJECT | Requested change |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Critic: Formalist (Greenberg) | Modernist Painting (Clement Greenberg): each medium earns its authority by being purely itself, which here means the flat plane, the grid and the type are exact. A page that is "nearly aligned" has not committed to its own surface. | The template is a real advance: one column at 364-1068 px, numbered sections, a single green-ramp vocabulary and calm 8 px radii. It still is not exact. On the live build the Basics figure clips its own label. The Contracts arrowheads pile into a black blob. The home page has two left edges (40 and 80 px). The guide-card titles sit at three different heights (694/704/715 px). The sidebar map has three different "you are here" styles and one label that collides with its outline. Hashes wrap to 1-2 character orphans. Figure type renders larger than body text on desktop. Also, the evidence screenshots are stale: they show copy ("needed to finalize") and an unclipped Basics label that the served site no longer has. | OBJECT | Fix defects 1-8 below. Each is a one-line change in _src/figures.py or site/css/site.css. Then reshoot the evidence from the live build. |

## Defects, most visible first

1. **Basics, hero figure: label clipped (live site, both widths).** "becomes the next “previous”" overruns the 360-unit viewBox by 14 px at 1280 and 10 px at 375, so it renders as "becomes the next “previou". The R6 screenshot hides this because it is stale. Fix in `_src/figures.py` line 55: shorten the label to `"the next “previous”"`, or keep the wording and wrap it into two `<tspan>` lines at x=190.
2. **Contracts figure: arrowheads collide.** All four lower arrows end at the same point (180,216), so four markers overprint into one black blob on top of "The same new state". Fix in `_src/figures.py` line 98: fan the targets out with `arrow(fid, x + 39, 168, 126 + i * 36, 216)`.
3. **Home: two left edges.** The hero (kicker, h1, CTA) starts at x=40 because `.hero{max-width:1280px}` (site.css line 72). "The guide, in reading order", the cards and the facts start at x=80 (`.home-sec{max-width:1200px}`). The header brand and the footer also sit at 40 while every inner page's sidebar and glossary sit at 80. Fix: set `.hero{max-width:1200px}`, and give the header and footer inner wrappers the same `max-width:1200px; margin:0 auto` so one left edge runs down every page.
4. **Home guide cards: staggered titles and mixed badges.** In row 1 the titles' tops are at 694, 704, 704 and 715 px. The cause is `.card-t{align-self:end}` (line 334) against a badge spanning 2 rows, so each title drops by however much its description wraps. Fix: `.card a{align-content:start}`, `.card-t{align-self:start}`, `.card-n{grid-row:1/3;align-self:start}`. The badges also use four border treatments: none, 1px `--rule`, 1px `--muted` and 2px `--ink` (`.c-bridge`). Use 1px `--rule` on every unfilled badge.
5. **Sidebar mini-map (every inner page).** (a) The active level has three different looks: a filled green rectangle (Basics), a white diamond with a 2px ink outline (Keys), and a coloured diamond with an ink outline (Consensus, Contracts, Layer 2). (b) On Contracts, the 800-weight "Smart contracts" label is as wide as the diamond at its baseline and runs into the 2px outline. (c) The bridge "↔" box (figures.py line 156, y = yb-40 to yb-6) floats 12px above its dashed connector (line 155, y = yb+6), so the two read as unconnected parts. Fix: drop the ink stroke (`.mini a[aria-current] .sl-t{stroke:none}`, line 333) and mark the active level by fill plus `.mn-t.on`. Set `.mn-t.on{font-size:12px}`. Draw the connector at the box's mid-height, `M{cx+hw},{yb-23} L164,{yb-23}`, so it meets the box.
6. **Top nav moves between pages.** The active link gets `font-weight:700` (line 64), which widens it and shifts every link to its left by up to 5px (Basics sits at x=560, 561, 562 or 565 depending on the page). Fix: remove `font-weight:700`, since the 3px green underline already marks the page, or reserve the bold width with an `a::after{content:attr(data-t);font-weight:700;height:0;visibility:hidden;display:block}`.
7. **64-character hashes wrap to orphans at 1280.** Examples: "00", "2c", "e4", "1f" and "e9" alone on a line in Break the chain; "dd434d1" in the hash demo; "cac56f8" in Mine; "36c5b18" in the Merkle root. Fix: `.hx code{max-width:32ch}` (line 137) so each hash splits evenly 32/32. Also, the Nonce label is 14px while the Hash label below it is 16px, because `.hx.big` scales the label too. Add `.hx.big span{font-size:.875rem}`.
8. **Figure type does not match body type.** `.fig-svg{max-width:440px}` on a 360-unit viewBox scales the figures 1.22x on desktop, so the figure labels render at 17-18px bold, above the 16px body text and the 15px caption. On mobile the scale is 0.88x, so text renders at 12.3px, against the figures.py docstring's "never under 13 px" (which also says "capped at 480"). Fix: `.fig-svg{max-width:360px}` (scale 1.0) and raise `.fg-s` and `.fg-m` to 15px, which gives at least 13.2px at 375.
9. **"One template" is not one template.** Chains, Risks, Autheo and About have no figure. Autheo and About also have no read-further block and no pager. Glossary drops the sidebar, puts its text block at 80-1040 instead of the 364-1068 column, and its Previous card (80-555) overshoots its left column (80-540). Fix: add the pager to Autheo and About in `doc()` (build.py); give the glossary `.pager` `grid-template-columns:repeat(2,minmax(0,460px));gap:40px` to match the column grid; add at least a small figure to Chains (the four-chain parts matrix) and to Risks.
10. **Keys demo: three left edges in one panel.** The "1." and "2." markers sit at x=386, the step labels, inputs and buttons at 403, and the Public key/Signature rows at 381. The disabled buttons (grey fill with white text) look like a different button family from the black primaries and the green CTA. Fix: `.steps{padding-left:0;list-style-position:inside}` so everything aligns at 381, and make disabled buttons `opacity:.45` on the primary style rather than a separate grey fill.
11. **Awkward wraps that a designer would set by hand.** Bridges "In one line" leaves "it." alone on its line. The Chains lede breaks "well-/known" at 1280. The Chains kicker at 375 leaves "DIFFERENTLY" alone. The Break the chain note breaks "“Re-/link”". The home fact 2 at 375 ends on "3 / zeros.". Fix: `.idea,.lede,.kicker,.demo-note,.facts li{text-wrap:pretty}` and a non-breaking hyphen (U+2011) in "Re‑link" and "well‑known" in content.py.
12. **Small residue.** (a) Glossary letter row at 375 wraps 6+6+1, leaving "W" alone. Use `.letters{display:grid;grid-template-columns:repeat(auto-fill,minmax(44px,1fr))}`, or 7 columns with a 5px gap. (b) In the Chains table, the first-column names are 14px bold while the other cells are 16px regular, so the baselines step by 2px. "Cosmos Hub" and "Scales or connects by" wrap in narrow columns, and the mobile label drops "by". (c) The figure legend says "must sign" and "under ⅓" while the table and demo say "Needed to finalize" and "fewer than ⅓". Pick one wording (figures.py lines 85-86).
