Three facts, computed

  • SHA-256("blockchain") begins ef7797e13d3a, and changing one letter gives 625da44e4eaf. Try it.
  • For the block “Ada pays Ben 5” to start with 3 zeros, a miner has to try 1,527 nonces. Mine it.
  • With 100 equal validators, a BFT chain finalizes a block once 67 agree, and stays safe with up to 33 faulty. Why.
