Three facts, computed

  • SHA-256("blockchain") begins ef7797e13d3a, and changing one letter gives 625da44e4eaf. Try it.
  • A chain of 4 blocks ends at 2768724a429a. Edit any block and the link after it breaks. Break it.
  • With 100 equal validators, CometBFT finalizes a block once 67 agree, and stays safe with up to 33 faulty. Why.
