The same parts, put together differently

Major chains compared

Every chain is a choice of consensus, execution and connections. Four well-known ones, and a case study, side by side.

In one lineEvery chain mixes the same parts; the mix decides what it is good at.

01Four chains

Launch year of the main network; consensus as of 2026
ChainLaunchedConsensusProgramsScales or connects by
Bitcoin2009Proof of workSmall fixed scriptsLayer 2 (Lightning)
Ethereum2015Proof of stake (since 2022)General contracts (EVM)Layer 2 rollups, bridges
Cosmos Hub2019Proof of stake, BFT voting (CometBFT)Modules (Cosmos SDK)IBC
Solana2020Proof of stake, with a clock called proof of historyGeneral programsBridges

Bitcoin is built to be money and changes slowly. Ethereum is built for contracts and scales through layer 2s. Cosmos is a family of separate chains, each built with the Cosmos SDK on CometBFT and joined by IBC. Solana runs one fast chain and asks more of the computers that run it.

02A case study

Autheo describes itself as combining several of these parts: Cosmos SDK, CometBFT and an EVM. Its page sets that description against this guide, as an exercise in reading a project's claims.
